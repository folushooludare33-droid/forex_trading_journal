# Forex Trading Journal

## Project Overview
This project is a personal trading journal to track and analyze my forex trades. The goal is to maintain discipline and evaluate the performance of my trading strategy with strict risk management.

## Trading Rules & Strategy
- **Risk-Reward Ratio**: 1:4
- **Win Rate**: ~60%
- **Risk per Trade**: 0.5% of account balance ($25 on a $5k account)
- **Instrument Traded**: EUR/USD
- **Strategy**: A 1 minute price action view in accordance to the 4 hour time frame direction

## Data Dictionary
- `Trade#`: Sequential trade number
- `Date`: Date of trade execution
- `Direction`: Bullish (Long) or Bearish (Short)
- `Entry/Exit Price`: Price at which the trade was opened and closed
- `P&L`: Profit or Loss in USD
- `Win/Loss`: Outcome of the trade

## Key Performance Metrics (from my dataset)
- **Total Trades** 22
- **Win Rate**: ~60.67% (Outperforming target 30%)
- **Profit Factor**: ~4.0 (Total Gross Profit / Total Gross Loss)
- **Max Drawdown**: $0 (Not yet realized)
